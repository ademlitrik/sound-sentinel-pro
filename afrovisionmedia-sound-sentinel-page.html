Enterprise Value Proposition

Seven Core Architectural Pillars

Engineered for security service providers, healthcare clinics, and privacy-conscious organizations.

100% Edge Privacy

Microphone audio never leaves physical device memory. Raw voice recordings are never streamed, stored, or processed on external cloud networks.

Zero Subscription Model

Commercial surveillance platforms charge monthly subscription fees per sensor. Sound Sentinel Pro is provided 100% free with unlimited devices.

521 Acoustic Classes

Pre-trained on Google's AudioSet taxonomy to detect alarms, weapon discharges, forced entry, pediatric cries, machinery anomalies, and liquid leaks.

Sub-15ms Latency

Because audio is classified locally in RAM, alerts fire instantaneously in milliseconds without suffering cloud round-trip network delays.

Scientific FFT Telemetry
