System Objective & Mission Rationale

Developed specifically for advanced home security surveillance and scientific acoustic research. Sound Sentinel leverages local deep neural network inference to classify domestic acoustic patterns without transmitting raw audio to third-party cloud servers, guaranteeing absolute privacy and real-time perimeter threat intelligence.
